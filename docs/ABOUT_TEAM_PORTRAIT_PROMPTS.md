## Prompt 1

Use case: identity-preserve. Edit the supplied real employee photo into a premium but friendly black-and-white studio portrait for the DHIRA company team page. The supplied person is the EDIT TARGET, not a style reference. Preserve their recognisable identity: exact facial proportions, age, eyes, eyebrows, nose, smile/expression, hairline, facial hair, glasses and headwear where present. Do not beautify into another person; preserve natural skin texture and asymmetry. DSLR-quality editorial photography, eye-level 85mm-equivalent lens, soft large key light from upper camera-left with gentle fill, crisp eyes, realistic fine detail, no artificial skin smoothing. Consistent set specification: portrait 4:5 aspect ratio, a single person centred, same camera distance, full head and both shoulders down to upper chest. Top of head or headwear at 8% of image height, pupils at approximately 34%, chin at approximately 56%; shoulders at 67%, upper chest at lower edge. Face should be approximately 40% of image width, with slight natural variation for anatomy. Straighten perspective and level eye line, comfortable natural posture facing near camera, arms/hands entirely outside crop. Plain even pale grey studio backdrop, no visible objects, scenery, texture or gradients. Strictly monochrome black and white. Keep the person's original clothing style, with natural professional neatness; preserve headwear and glasses rather than imagining what is underneath. Remove distracting carried equipment/backpack/headphones if visible, keep identity features. No text, names, logo, border, watermark, frame, decorative effects, vignette or dramatic rim lighting. The deliverable is one full-frame photographic portrait, not a contact sheet.
